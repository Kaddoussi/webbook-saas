FAQs

Authors ask. We answer.

Do I keep my rights?

Yes — 100%. WebBook SaaS never claims authorship or ownership. You can export your manuscript and walk away anytime.

How long does it take to publish?

Under 5 minutes for a typical 200-page manuscript. The WebBook Agent does the heavy lifting.

Is the AI my co-author?

No. The agent is your assistant, never the author. It formats, translates, narrates, and powers the reader companion — but it doesn't write your book.

What does the free plan include?

One WebBook, up to 3 languages, audiobook included, AI companion enabled, custom subdomain. No credit card needed.

Can I bring my own domain?

Yes — on the Author plan and above. SSL and DNS handled by the agent.
