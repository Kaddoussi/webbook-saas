The concept

What is a WebBook?

A WebBook is a book that breathes. It speaks in your reader's language, narrates itself out loud, answers questions, evolves over time, and tells you exactly how it's being read.

A book that listens as much as it speaks.

Old books are one-way streets. WebBooks are conversations. Readers can highlight, ask, translate, and follow the author's thinking — without leaving the page.

  • AI companion — every WebBook ships with its own reading guide, tuned to the author's voice.
  • Multilingual — auto-translated into up to 40 languages, with the author's tone preserved.
  • Narrated — studio-quality audiobook generated automatically. Listen on the train.
  • Living — edit a paragraph, every reader sees the update. No re-uploads.
