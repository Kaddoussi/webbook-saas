Privacy

Last updated: April 2026

We treat your manuscript like the private craft it is. We don't train foundation models on your text. We store data in EU or US, your choice. You can export everything and delete your account anytime.

What we collect

Account email, billing info (handled by Stripe), and the manuscripts you upload. Reader analytics are aggregated and anonymized.

Sub-processors

Cloudflare (CDN), Stripe (payments), AWS (compute, storage, EU + US). Full list available on request.
