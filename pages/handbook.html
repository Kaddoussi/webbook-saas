Author handbook

Make a great WebBook.

A short, opinionated guide — what we've learned from thousands of WebBooks shipped.

1. Write for the ear, not the eye

Every WebBook becomes audio. Read your draft aloud. If it stumbles, rewrite it.

2. Title your chapters like songs

"Chapter 7" is a vibe-killer. "The morning the algorithm got bored" is a click.

3. Train your AI companion

Give it 5 example Q&As in your voice. It'll match the rest.

4. End every chapter with a question

Readers reply. Communities form. Your sequel writes itself.

5. Ship a "draft" version first

Living books are forgiving. Push to 50 trusted readers, learn, refine, then go public.
